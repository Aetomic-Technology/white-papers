## Our Approach

Aetomic approaches technology from an operational perspective.

We are interested not simply in what intelligent systems can do, but how they should operate in environments where information is incomplete, conditions change quickly, consequences matter, and humans remain responsible for decisions.

Several principles guide our work:

**Evidence over assumption.**  
Systems should distinguish what is known from what is inferred, assumed, or unknown.

**Human authority.**  
Artificial intelligence should support human judgement and command authority rather than silently replace it.

**Auditability.**  
Important assessments, decisions, state changes, and outcomes should be explainable and traceable.

**Operational usefulness.**  
Technology should improve real-world outcomes, not merely produce more information.
